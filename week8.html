What is Modigliani-Miller Proposition I (no taxes)?
Capital structure does not affect firm value: V_L = V_U. Debt has no benefit in a no-tax world.
Why does debt not add value in MM Proposition I?
No tax shield and investors can replicate leverage personally ("homemade leverage").
What is MM Proposition II (no taxes)?
As debt increases, cost of equity rises: r_e = r_0 + (B/S)(r_0 - r_d), but WACC remains the same.
What changes with MM when corporate taxes are introduced?
Debt creates value via tax shield: V_L = V_U + T * B. r_e rises more slowly due to tax benefits.
What is the revised MM Proposition II with taxes?
r_e = r_0 + (B/S)(r_0 - r_d)(1 - T). Equity gets riskier, but tax shield reduces the increase.
Why does WACC fall when you add debt (with taxes)?
Debt is cheaper than equity, and the tax shield reduces the total cost → WACC < r_0 → NPV increases.
What is the Optimal Capital Structure?
It’s the debt level that minimizes WACC and maximizes firm value, balancing tax benefits and distress costs.
What are costs of financial distress?
Direct (legal, admin) and indirect (underinvestment, poor stakeholder relations, risk-taking behavior).
What is Target Capital Structure (TCS)?
A long-term leverage mix chosen to support business strategy and used for valuation and decision-making.
Factors affecting Target Capital Structure?
FCF uncertainty, type of assets (intangible vs tangible), and tax rate.
How do you implement TCS?
Recapitalizations, tailored financing, and dividend policy adjustments.
What are the stylized facts from empirical evidence?
1. Most firms have <50% debt. 2. Capital structure varies by industry. 3. Most firms follow target ratios.
What’s Enterprise Value (EV)?
EV = sum of future Free Cash Flows discounted at WACC. Represents total firm value.
How do you get Equity Value from EV?
Equity Value = EV - Net Debt - Minority Interests + Non-core Assets
